work:
  heading: The Work
  paragraphs:
    - Severin has spent his career in the Hudson Valley — framing new houses, repairing old timber frames, managing complex renovations, and working on properties from modest farmhouses to significant historic landmarks. He knows how buildings go together, what they cost, and who does good work in this region.
    - Earlier in his career he also worked in New York City on interior renovations and ran a cabinetry business serving high-end interiors — work that sharpened his eye for detailing, millwork, and the coordination a project demands.
    - Across forty years, that has meant hundreds of structures and nearly every kind of building problem — and a deep, first-hand familiarity with the trades, materials, and methods particular to this part of the world.

    Severin is a descendant of the von Trapp family. His mother, **Johanna von Trapp**, was a daughter of Captain Georg von Trapp and one of the children who sang with the family on the concert tours later known the world over through *The Sound of Music.*

That inheritance still runs through the way Severin approaches his work today.
