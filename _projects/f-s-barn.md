Like so many of the timber frame barns that dot the Hudson Valley, this one had endured generations of hard use and stood in serious disrepair — and few of them are fortunate enough to receive a full restoration. Its owners decided this barn deserved one, and they made it a priority that the work honor the building's craft heritage, using traditional woodworking methods and practices throughout.

The restoration began by gutting the barn back to its core load-bearing timber structure. Compromised beams and posts were replaced with new ones, hand-carved by experts in traditional woodworking techniques and joined in the spirit of the original frame. Around that preserved skeleton, the barn was carefully updated for its next chapter as a guest apartment and a hall for events and gatherings — the hand-worked frame now on full display in a bright, open interior.
